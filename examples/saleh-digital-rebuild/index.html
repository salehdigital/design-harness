What is a design harness

A design harness is a collection of design records that agents read to understand my design system, visual foundations, and constraints.

It contains three parts: foundations (brand, typography, colors, spacing, themes), components (UI building blocks, patterns, screens), and intelligence (policies, memories, vocabulary).

The harness enables agents to design and build with my voice and constraints. Every decision is documented. Every token is explained. Nothing is arbitrary.

Why this matters

Agents need context to design well. Without a harness, they guess. With one, they know.

The system is small first. I add only what's necessary. Every element serves a purpose.
